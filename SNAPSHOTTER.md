# BinF Snapshotter Guide

If you haven't already, install `vs-code` with `platformio` or `pio-arduino` (the latter I have not personally used).

When installed correctly, you'll be able to select the env or see it among the tasks. Select the right one for your platform.

Plug in your badge and click `upload` or `upload and monitor`. **BEWARE**: this will erase everything currently on the device.

When it is finished, you can play this *Snapshot* of the development.

## Snapshot Info (WorldGen)

Unfortunately I was unable to get the full MVP\* ready at this time. *Development will continue!*

For this **Snapshot**, you can roam around an infinite world with one ore type currently spawning. The hard edges you see will later be removed, thanks to the game's auto-tiling.

**Controls**:

- Joystick: move around.
- button A: gain a speed boost while pressed.
- button B: regen a new world with a (kind of) random seed!
